PatchUp photo and location privacy

PatchUp is an independent community reporting initiative for adults. We collect photos of roadway potholes and the location you confirm to support repairs.

Photo processing

GPS is extracted in your browser when present. Please remove faces, license plates, house numbers and other personal details before uploading. We re-encode the photo on your device and server to remove embedded metadata. Google Cloud Sensitive Data Protection receives the metadata-free pothole photograph to mask detected faces and license plates. Gemini receives the resulting photograph and classification instructions, not your location, notes or account identity. If redaction is unavailable, the photo cannot be saved or verified. Profile photographs are separate and are not sent to these services. Visible details can still reveal a location or identity. Approved photos and pothole locations may be visible to the public and included in reports to ATL311.

Location and account data

We use a confirmed map pin, one-time GPS or photo GPS, and a street name to locate the pothole. We do not track location continuously. Google Maps displays the map. With your optional, revocable Capture consent, Google receives the selected pin coordinates for a private approximate address preview. That preview is not stored or included in community records or report exports. Independent street data or your independent observation supplies the reporting location. An optional city street data service may receive coordinates. See Google’s Privacy Policy and PatchUp terms. Your account and private submissions are stored separately from public views. Avoid adding personal information in private notes.

Retention and control

Approved evidence may be retained to document community reports, including after account deletion with account identifiers removed. Unsubmitted and rejected photos are scheduled for cleanup; pending reviews remain until resolved. You may delete your account through PatchUp. Contact the team through its published support channel for privacy requests. This notice should be reviewed and supplemented with the team's contact details before public launch.
